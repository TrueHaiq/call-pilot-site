# Positioning

Working document. It defines what Call Pilot is, who it's for, and why it wins. Copy, page structure and sales material should trace back to it. Edit it on purpose as decisions change.

- **Status:** v0.1 draft, 2026-11-11. Nothing here is final until approved.
- **Evidence:** [research report](../reports/Aircall%20and%20Israeli%20voice%20AI%20market.md), with notes in `research_notes/Aircall and Israeli voice AI market/`.
- **Name:** "Call Pilot" is a working name (see [BRAND.md](BRAND.md#naming)).

## 1. Positioning in one line

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Category** | AI-first customer communications platform |
| **Hebrew** | פלטפורמת תקשורת הלקוחות מבוססת ה-AI של ישראל |
| **English** | The AI-first customer communications platform built for Israel |

**Positioning statement:** for mid-market Israeli companies whose customers call and message all day, Call Pilot is the AI-first customer communications platform that answers, understands and follows up in natural Hebrew. It covers phone, WhatsApp and SMS, and every conversation lands in one customer view. Aircall treats Israel as one of 38 languages, and local vendors sell a bot as a project. Call Pilot is a complete product built for how Israeli businesses work.

## 2. Who it's for

### Primary: mid-market teams

Companies with roughly 10 to 200 people handling customer conversations in support, sales, bookings or service, and a phone line that's busy every day. Typical sectors: insurance agencies, healthcare and clinic networks, real estate and property management, e-commerce and retail, automotive (dealers and service centers), professional services, utilities and municipal-style service centers.

| Persona | Cares about | What they need to believe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Head of customer service / CX | Answer rate, wait times, quality, team load | "It handles the routine calls well and hands off the rest with context." |
| VP sales / revenue ops | Lead response time, qualification, follow-up | "No lead waits, and every call ends up in the CRM." |
| COO / CEO | Cost per conversation, staffing, risk | "It's predictable, compliant, and doesn't embarrass us." |
| IT / security | Data, integrations, permissions | "Data is handled to Israeli law and it plugs into our systems." |

### Secondary: service SMBs

Clinics, law firms, insurance agents, agencies and garages. The site doesn't speak to them first, but they should recognize themselves through use cases and simple pricing. The long-term vision is mid-to-large companies, so the brand should feel built for serious operations from day one.

## 3. The problem we solve

In the buyer's words, not ours:

- Calls go unanswered at the busiest hours (mornings, evenings, Shabbat), and an unanswered call leaves no trace in any report.
- The team spends its day on the same ten questions instead of the conversations that need a person.
- Phone, WhatsApp and SMS live in different places, so nobody sees the whole customer.
- After a call, someone still has to write the summary, update the CRM and send the follow-up, and often nobody does.
- Global tools don't really speak Hebrew, and local bots feel like a project, not a product.

## 4. What Call Pilot is (product pillars)

| Pillar | What it does | Confirmed scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **AI voice agents** | Answer and make calls: answer questions, qualify, book, route, and hand off to a person with a summary | Inbound and outbound; Hebrew and English, including switching mid-call |
| **AI messaging agents** | The same intelligence on WhatsApp and SMS, including follow-up after a call | WhatsApp and SMS at launch |
| **Phone system** | Israeli numbers and calling as part of the product | We provide numbers; porting/forwarding to confirm |
| **Customer dashboard (CRM)** | One timeline per customer: calls, recordings, transcripts, summaries, messages, tags, tasks | Core of the product |
| **Automations** | After-call actions: update the CRM, send a WhatsApp, create a task, book a meeting | Rules engine; scope to confirm |
| **Integrations** | Israeli CRMs and calendars | Fireberry/Powerlink, Priority, Monday.com; Google/Outlook calendars |

**Structural difference against Aircall:** Aircall isn't a CRM; it syncs contacts from HubSpot or Salesforce. Call Pilot has its own customer view, so every conversation becomes a customer record without another tool.

## 6. Messaging hierarchy

### Lead message (hero)

Aircall's angle, a central AI-first communications platform, made local and calmer. Hebrew is written first and the English is adapted.

**Chosen direction: C, "built for Israel".** Refine the wording during design. "Smart" (חכמה) is a weak word, so make the subhead carry the specifics.

| # | Hebrew | English | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **C** | **תקשורת לקוחות חכמה, שנבנתה לישראל.** | Smart customer communication, built for Israel. | **Chosen.** Clear category and local claim; relies on the subhead and product visual for memorability |
| A | שירות הלקוחות שלכם, על טייס אוטומטי. ואתם בשליטה. | Customer service on autopilot. With you in control. | Alternative; the autopilot idea can still appear in a later section |
| B | כל שיחה עם לקוח, במקום אחד. מנוהלת על ידי AI שמדבר עברית. | Every customer conversation in one place, handled by AI that speaks Hebrew. | Alternative; good line for the dashboard section |

Subhead draft (HE): סוכני AI שעונים לשיחות ולהודעות בווטסאפ, מבינים עברית אמיתית, ומעדכנים כל לקוח במערכת. הצוות שלכם מטפל רק במה שבאמת צריך אדם.

Subhead draft (EN): AI agents answer calls and WhatsApp messages, understand real Hebrew, and log every customer in one place. Your team only handles what truly needs a person.

### Supporting pillars (sections of the page)

1. **Speaks real Hebrew.** Slang, interruptions, names, addresses, mixed Hebrew and English. Proof: recordings and later a public Hebrew quality test.
2. **Every conversation in one place.** Phone, WhatsApp and SMS on one customer timeline, with summaries and next steps.
3. **Work that finishes itself.** After every call: CRM updated (Fireberry, Priority, Monday), follow-up sent, meeting booked.
4. **Built for Israel.** Israeli numbers, ₪ pricing with VAT stated, a Hebrew-speaking team, privacy law (Amendment 13) and accessibility handled.
5. **You stay in control.** The agent only answers from knowledge you approve, says when it doesn't know, and hands off to a person with full context. We name hallucination directly; nobody else does.

### Claims to avoid as headlines

Everyone in the category says these, so they're supporting specs at most, never the lead: "sounds human", "native Hebrew", "24/7", "never miss a call", "X% accuracy", "under X ms", "live in minutes", "AI secretary", "replaces your staff", "the future of CX". Don't publish accuracy percentages we can't back with a public method.

## 8. Proof plan

There's no customer proof yet, so the page design keeps **slots** for it and never fills them with invented content.

- **Now:** product proof only: real dashboard UI, real (or clearly labeled sample) transcripts, compliance facts.
- **Next:** 3 to 5 design-partner customers in priority sectors to produce named logos, one quote each, and one outcome metric each (answer rate, response time, hours saved).
- **Later:** a live Hebrew demo number, playable recordings per sector, and a public Hebrew quality benchmark.

## 9. Pricing and CTA

- **Model:** per seat (human users) + AI usage (minutes / conversations). Public, in ₪, VAT stated ("לא כולל מע״מ" or "כולל מע״מ"), Israeli tax invoices.
- **Price points (draft, set 2026-11-11, in the design; excl. VAT, billed annually, monthly billing +20%):**

  | Plan | Per user / month | AI minutes included (per account) | Extra minute | Minimum users | First agent built with you |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | התחלה | ₪169 | 200 | ₪0.99 | 1 | ₪990 one-time |
  | צמיחה (recommended) | ₪289 | 1,000 | ₪0.89 | 3 | Included |
  | ארגונים | Custom | Volume package | from ₪0.69 | 15 | Included, dedicated manager |

  Assumes an all-in cost of about ₪0.30–0.40 per AI minute (speech recognition, language model, voice output, telephony). Confirm real unit cost before launch; if it is higher, raise the extra-minute price first.
- **Recommendations to decide:** no 3-seat minimum (Aircall's top pricing complaint); include the customer dashboard and WhatsApp follow-up in every plan rather than selling them as add-ons; show an "Enterprise" tier for SSO, data residency and SLAs.
- **Primary CTA:** "Book a demo" (קביעת הדגמה / תאמו הדגמה, wording to finalize). The demo promise: we build your first agent with you, in Hebrew.
- **Secondary CTA (for now):** "See pricing". Later: "Hear a call" once recordings or a demo number exist.
- **Lead form:** marketing consent as a separate, unticked checkbox (spam law).

## 10. Compliance as part of the story

Not a footnote: it's a selling point in a market where few vendors take it seriously. Product claims we can make only once true:

- Recording and AI notice at the start of every call, on by default.
- Retention and auto-delete settings; deletion by customer, with an audit log.
- Outbound consent tracking and do-not-call list (spam law, §30A).
- No training on customer data.
- Landing page: IS 5568 (WCAG AA) with a Hebrew accessibility statement, a Hebrew privacy policy covering Amendment 13, and cookie disclosure.

Outbound marketing calls and AI-disclosure duties need confirmation from Israeli counsel before launch.
